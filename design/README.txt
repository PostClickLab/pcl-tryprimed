PRIMED — Telemedicine design concept

The primary conversion is an online consultation. Treatment options are secondary and require individual clinical review.

mockups/: Complete desktop/mobile design board.
assets/photos/: Standalone generated editorial photography.
assets/products/: Concept packaging and treatment-format images. Vials use transparent backgrounds.
assets/icons/: Editable SVG interface icons.
assets/primed-wordmark.svg: Editable concept wordmark.

All imagery is generated for this design concept. Professional images do not depict verified PRIMED staff. Quotes shown in the mockups are illustrative and must be replaced with genuine approved testimonials before publication. Packaging, treatment availability, formats and clinical copy require confirmation against the actual service. Individual assets follow the mockup art direction; image-generated layouts are visual concepts rather than pixel-exact production specifications.

Palette: Navy #08264C; Teal #009BAD; Pale blue #EAF6FB; White #FFFFFF.
Mobile intent: stacked hero, full-width consultation buttons, horizontally scrollable goal/treatment cards, stacked care steps, concise FAQ and footer.
